Jimny tuning FAQ

How much power does a Suzuki Jimny make after Stage 2?

On our measured JB74W (K15B 1.5, 4AT) the Stage 2 tune went from 105 PS to 115 PS and from 134 Nm to 148 Nm, with 12 Nm more at 3,000 rpm, where you drive most. Figures are crank-referenced from a road dyno run at 1,450 m in Bir.

What does a Jimny Stage 2 include?

An in-house recalibration of the Bosch ME17.9.64 ECU (fuel, ignition and torque model) written over OBD with Alientech Kess3, a BMC Racing high-flow intake filter, a free-flow catalyst-back exhaust and revised throttle response mapping, then road-test validation.

Can you tune an automatic Jimny?

Yes. The Jimny on our dyno card is a 4-speed automatic.

Do I have to bring my Jimny to Bir?

Most customers drive in to our garage in Bir, Himachal Pradesh, including from Chandigarh, Delhi NCR and Punjab. Message us on WhatsApp on +91 90150 55636 to plan your slot.

Will a remap affect my warranty?

A remap changes the factory calibration, which can affect manufacturer warranty claims on the engine. Ask us before booking and we will explain exactly what changes on your car.
